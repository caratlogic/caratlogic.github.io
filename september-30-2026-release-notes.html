Inventory & Transactions — Release Notes

This release makes the single-line PDF available in every region, opens Memo Out on Not Returned memos, adds Stock ID search across transactions, and fixes the diamond colour shown on the public page.

September 30, 2026
01
📄
New

Single-Line PDF Now Available in All Regions

You can now choose the consolidated single-line PDF on Invoice, Proforma, Quotation and Memo Out for every region. Before this release it was India only.

  • Outside India, a single-line PDF shows one Tax (x%) total row and no HSN or GST tax table.
  • India keeps its CGST, SGST and IGST breakdown.
Fix: On a single-line PDF with no services or miscellaneous items, the tax and total now match the regular invoice exactly. Previously small rounding differences could appear.
02
📦
Changed

Memo Out Opens on Not Returned Memos

The Memo Out list now shows only Not Returned memos by default. To see Returned or Cancelled memos, pick them in the Status filter.

03
🔍
New

Search by Stock ID in Transactions

You can now search by a gem or diamond Stock ID (partial match, not case-sensitive) in:

ScreenStatus
Proforma✅ New
Invoice✅ New
Quotation✅ New
Memo Out✅ New
Memo Out Returns✅ New
Memo In✅ Already available
Memo In Returns✅ New (the Returned tab now has a search box)
04
💎
Fixed

Diamond Colour on the Public (Shared) Page

The public diamond page now shows the normal Color Grade (D, E, F and so on), the same as the private detail page.

Also: when a diamond import sets both Color Grade and Fancy Color, Fancy Color is used and the import review shows a warning under Color Grade.
