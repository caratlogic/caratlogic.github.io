Release Notes

Platform-wide dark mode, a complete UI redesign, rebuilt dashboard, refreshed inventory and financial workflows, and expanded media export.

June 6, 2026
01
🌙
New

Dark Mode Across the Entire Platform

Dark mode is now available across all CaratLogic modules, providing a consistent viewing experience throughout the application.

  • Switch between Light and Dark mode using the theme toggle in the top navigation
  • Theme preference is automatically saved and restored on future sign-ins
  • Supported across inventory, financial operations, business contacts, partner management, exhibitions, settings, and shared inventory pages
  • Public gem and diamond detail links also support dark mode
Users can now work comfortably in different lighting conditions without sacrificing usability or consistency.
