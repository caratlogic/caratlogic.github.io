Hold Inventory — Release Notes

This release adds Hold, a way to set stones aside for a customer or anyone else without creating a memo out or invoice. Held carats stay in your shop, but nobody can invoice them, memo them out or hold them again until you convert the hold into a memo out or invoice, release it, or it runs out. A hold lasts from 1 to 30 days, or until you release it.

November 22, 2026
01
⏳
New

Hold Inventory

Where: Sidebar → Financial Operations → Hold

When a customer asks you to keep a stone for them, put it on hold. The held carats are reserved for that hold, so they can't be sold, memoed out or put on another hold by mistake.

  • Each hold gets its own number, e.g. HL-0001.
  • Hold stones for a customer, or for someone else by name, such as a broker or a colleague.
  • Hold a whole stone, or part of the carats of a parcel or set.
  • Make a hold temporary (1 to 30 days) or permanent (until you release it).
  • When the customer decides, convert the hold into a memo out or an invoice, or release it so the carats go back to stock.
  • Up to 500 stones can be on one hold.
Held stones are still in your shop. They count as available stock and show in a new On Hold column on your inventory lists (see 10).

Create a Hold

Where: Hold → + Hold, or from your inventory (see 03)

StepWhat you do
1. CustomerChoose Customer and search for the customer, or click Add new customer. Or choose Someone else and type a name or reference in Hold for (up to 200 characters).
2. ItemsPick stones under Gems and Diamonds. Only stones with free carats are listed. For a parcel or set, Carats to hold starts at all its free carats; lower it to hold only part, and enter the number of pieces in Qty if you like. Single and pair stones are held whole.
3. Hold DurationLeave Permanent hold off and enter Duration (days), from 1 to 30. The date it lasts until is shown below, e.g. Held until 25 Nov, 2026. Or turn Permanent hold on: it stays on hold until released or converted.
4. Additional NotesOptional notes for the hold.

Click Submit & review. The Review & Confirm Hold page shows the customer, the line items with their quantity and carats, the hold type, duration and held-until date, and your notes. Click Edit on any section to go back to it, or Confirm & Create Hold to create the hold.

  • Carats to hold must be above 0 and no more than the stone's free carats, e.g. Only 1.25 ct available.
  • A stone can be added once per hold. Adding it again shows Item already added.

How Long a Hold Lasts

Temporary holdPermanent hold
Duration1 to 30 daysNot applicable
Held untilThe end of the day that is the hold date plus the duration. A 3-day hold placed on 18 Nov is held until the end of 21 Nov.Until released
When it endsReleased automatically. The held carats go back to stock.Never ends on its own. Release or convert it when you're done.
  • Days are counted in your organization's timezone, so a hold ends at midnight where you are.
  • A hold always lasts at least the number of days you chose, measured in full 24-hour days.
  • Holds are released automatically within the hour after they end. Until then, the hold list marks them Overdue.

Hold Details

Where: Hold → click a Hold #

  • Carats on hold at the top, with a bar showing how much was Released, went on a Memo out, was Invoiced, and is Still on hold, e.g. 60% settled · 5.00 ct held.
  • The hold date, held-until date, duration, type and number of items.
  • The customer's details, including address and tax number, or the name the hold is for.
  • Held items: each stone with its own status, quantity and carats. Converted to lists the memo outs and invoices that took part of it, with the carats each took, e.g. INV-0042 · 1.50 ct. A stone that was partly converted shows what's still held, e.g. 0.75 left.
  • Your notes.

Tick stones that are still on hold and open Action to Convert to Memo Out, Convert to Invoice, Release selected or Remove selected.

Convert a Hold to a Memo Out or Invoice

When the customer decides, turn the hold into a memo out or invoice. Create Memo Out or Create Invoice opens with the hold's customer and stones already filled in.

ConvertHow
The whole holdChoose Convert to Memo Out or Convert to Invoice from the hold list or the hold's menu. Every stone still on hold is added at its held carats.
Some stonesOn Hold Details, tick the stones, then Action → Convert to Memo Out or Convert to Invoice. In the dialog, enter the carats to convert for each parcel or set; it starts at the held carats. Single and pair stones are Converted whole. Click Continue.
  • On the memo out or invoice, a stone's line can use its held carats plus any free carats it has.
  • Save the memo out or invoice as usual. The carats it takes come off the hold, and the document is listed under Converted to on the hold.
  • Convert less than a stone's held carats, and the rest stays on hold for this customer. You can convert it later, or release it.
  • Once nothing is left on hold, the hold closes as Invoiced or Memo out.
⚠ Only customer holds can be converted. The memo out or invoice must be for the hold's customer. A hold for someone else can be edited or released, but not converted.

Edit a Hold

Where: Hold → Edit on a hold's row, or in its menu on Hold Details

Edit Hold opens as a full page, for holds that are still on hold.

  • Customer: who the hold is for can't be changed (Customer cannot be edited).
  • Items: add more stones, the same way as on Create Hold. Remove a stone that's still fully on hold; it's marked Will be removed until you save. A stone can only be on a hold once (This stone is already on this hold).
  • Duration: enter a new duration, or switch Permanent hold on or off. A permanent hold made temporary starts at 1 day.
  • Notes: change your notes.

Click Save changes. You'll see Hold updated.

A new duration counts from the day you save it, not from the original hold date. Changing a hold to 2 days on 20 Nov holds it until the end of 22 Nov. Saving only notes or items leaves the end date as it is.
⚠ An ended hold needs a new duration before you add stones. If you add stones to a hold that has ended but isn't released yet, you'll see This hold has expired. Set a new duration to add items.

Release a Hold

Release puts held carats back into stock, ready to sell.

  • Release all, on the hold list or Hold Details, releases every stone still on hold.
  • Release selected, on Hold Details, releases only the stones you ticked.
  • The dialog lists each stone with the carats going back, e.g. 3 items · 4.20 ct back to stock.

Release or remove?

A hold must keep at least one stone. To clear a hold completely, release it.

Held Carats Are Protected

While carats are on hold, only that hold can use them. Everywhere else, CaratLogic tells you why a stone can't be used.

When youWhat happens
Create an invoice, memo out or proforma from a stoneIf every carat is on hold, a Can't create invoice dialog explains that all carats of this stone are on hold. Convert or release the hold first.
Create an invoice or memo out for selected stonesStones with every carat on hold are left out and listed under All carats are on hold, e.g. 2 stones will be left out. You choose a customer for the rest.
Pick a stone on an invoice or memo outA stone fully on hold is shown but can't be picked: Can't be invoiced or Can't be memoed out. A stone partly on hold can be picked, with a warning, e.g. Stock ID G1001: 0.50 of 2.00 ct are on hold. Only 1.50 ct can be invoiced.
Delete or archive a stoneNot allowed while carats are on hold: Cannot delete: carats are on hold. Release or convert the hold first. Held stones are left out of bulk archive.
Split or merge a parcelNot allowed while any of its carats are on hold.
Converting a hold is the exception: on the memo out or invoice it creates, the hold's stones can use their held carats (see 07).
