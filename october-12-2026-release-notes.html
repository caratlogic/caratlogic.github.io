Inventory Search — Release Notes

Gem and diamond inventory get two new ways to find stock: a filter row under every column, and a full-page Advanced Search with counts on every option. Filters show as chips you can remove one at a time, and you can save any combination as a search to use again.

October 12, 2026
01
⚙
New

Column Filters: Filter Right in the Table

Where: Gem Inventory or Diamond Inventory → Column filters

Click Column filters to show a filter row under the table headers. Each column gets the right kind of filter for its data, and the list updates as you type or pick.

  • The row is hidden until you open it, and it starts hidden each time the page loads. Hiding it doesn't remove your filters.
  • The button shows how many column filters are on, e.g. Hide column filters  2. Each filtered column shows a small funnel icon in its header.
  • To remove every column filter at once, click the funnel icon at the left end of the row (Clear all column filters).
  • Columns that can't be filtered show an empty, dashed cell. On diamonds, Diamond Type is set by the Natural / Lab-Grown tab, and Growth Type only appears on Lab-Grown.

Pick from a list

Columns such as Shape, Status, Stock Type, Gem Type, Color, Color Grade, Fancy Color, Clarity, Cut, Polish, Symmetry, Certificate Type, Origin Country, Supplier, Ownership and Warehouse show All. Click it to choose one or more values.

  • Each value shows how many stones have it, highest first. Only values that exist in your inventory are listed.
  • Long lists have a search box (e.g. Search shapes) and a Clear link. Short lists show as pills.
  • Picking several values shows stones that match any of them. The cell then reads e.g. Round +1.

Type a number or a rule

Number columns (carats, prices, discounts, measurements, quantities, Depth %, Table %, Ratio and more) take a value or a rule. Click the box to see examples for that column, and click one to use it.

  • If the app can't read what you typed, it says so and suggests a format, e.g. Try 1–3, >1 or <=3.

Paste a list of IDs

Stock ID, Caratlogic ID and Certificate # have a Paste IDs… box. Paste a list straight from Excel or an email.

  • Commas, semicolons, spaces and new lines all split IDs. Duplicates are removed.
  • Each ID shows as a pill, with a total such as 5 IDs. IDs that aren't in your inventory are simply not shown.
  • Stock IDs must match in full, in any letter case.

Other columns

  • Text columns (e.g. Description, Notes, Member Comments, Purchase, Fluorescence Color) show Contains… and match any part of the text.
  • Date columns (Created At / Date Added, Updated) show Any date. Pick Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This month or This fiscal year, or set your own From and To dates.
  • Active shows Any, Yes or No.

See Every Filter as a Chip

As soon as a filter is on, a Filtered by row appears above the table. Each filter is a chip with the column name and its value, e.g. Shape: Round, Oval or Total Carat: 1–2.

  • Click × on a chip to remove just that filter.
  • Clear all removes every filter. It keeps what you typed in the search box; Reset all in the toolbar clears that too.
  • Save search at the end of the row keeps this combination for later (see below).
  • Every way of filtering adds chips here: the column row, Advanced Search, the Ownership and Status buttons, and saved searches.
  • If nothing matches, you'll see No gems match these filters (or diamonds) and a Clear all filters button.

New

Advanced Search, Redesigned

Where: Gem Inventory or Diamond Inventory → Advance Search

Advance Search now opens a full page instead of a side panel. Filters are grouped into cards, every list shows how many stones have each value, and the button at the top tells you how many stones will match before you apply.

How it works

  • Choose filters on any card. Your choices appear as chips at the top, and the button updates to show the result, e.g. Show 611 diamonds.
  • Nothing changes in your list until you click Show …. To leave without applying, click the back arrow or press Esc.
  • Each card you've used has a Clear link. Clear all at the top clears everything.
  • You can load a saved search from Saved searches at the top, change it, then apply.

The cards

  • Find by ID — paste lists of Stock IDs and Certificate numbers.
  • Availability — Status (with counts), Stock type (Single, Parcel, Set, Pair) and Ownership (Owned, Partner, Memo In).
  • Price — switch between Selling, Cost and Retail, then set Price / ct, Total and Discount %. Diamonds also have Expense under Cost.
  • Carat — choose whether to Weigh by Available or Total carats.
  • Shape, Clarity, Treatment, Origin country, Lab, and Supplier & purchase (Supplier, Purchase ID, Price code).
  • Clarity is a range: click a start grade, then an end grade, e.g. VS1 – VS2. Grades follow the order set in your settings.
  • Every number has From and To boxes plus one-click Common sizes or Common ranges, such as 1.00–1.49 ct or a 10–20 discount. Click a selected one again to remove it.

Diamonds

  • Color has a White / Fancy switch: Color grade for white stones; Fancy color, Intensity and Overtone for fancy stones.
  • Extra cards for Cut & finish (Cut, Polish, Symmetry) and Fluorescence (Strength, Color). Growth type appears on the Lab-Grown tab.
  • Results and counts follow the Natural / Lab-Grown tab you're on.

Gems

  • A Gem type card, a Color card with Intensity, and gem clarity grades such as Eye Clean and Slightly Included.

More filters

Less-used filters are tucked under More filters at the bottom of the page. The button lists what's inside.

  • Quantities — Quantity (diamonds), In stock, Reserved (ct) and Sold (ct)
  • Measurements (mm) — Length, Breadth, Depth
  • Dates — Date added and Updated (Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This month, This fiscal year, or your own dates), and Active
  • Warehouse — Type and Warehouses
  • Caratlogic ID — paste a list
  • Diamonds: Proportions (Depth %, Table %, Ratio) and Rapnet (Price / ct, Discount %)
  • Gems: Color detail (Modifiers, Color system, Color description)
  • Notes, and your custom fields under Extra properties

Back in the list

Advanced Search and the column filters share the same filters. After you click Show …, your choices appear as chips and in the matching column filters, so you can fine-tune them right in the table.

Saved Searches

Where: Save search in the Filtered by row; Saved searches in the toolbar and on Advanced Search

Save any set of filters you use often, such as “Round & Oval 1–2 ct”, and bring it back in one click.

Save a search

  • Apply your filters, then click Save search. Give it a name (up to 100 characters). The dialog shows the filters being saved.
  • Your search box text is saved too. The page, sort order and the diamond Natural / Lab-Grown tab are not.
  • If the same filters are already saved, or the name is already used, you're asked before a second copy is created.

Use, rename and delete

  • Open Saved searches and click a search to apply it. It replaces your current filters.
  • Use Find a saved search to look up a search by its name or its filters.
  • Click the pencil to rename a search, or the bin to delete it. Deleting can't be undone. To change a search's filters, apply it, adjust, and save it again.
  • On Advanced Search, a saved search fills in the cards so you can adjust it before clicking Show ….
Saved searches are private to you. Gems and diamonds each keep their own list of up to 50. When you save a 51st, you're warned that your oldest saved search will be deleted.

Changed

Search Box Now Matches Whole IDs

The search box above the list now reads Search stock ID. It finds a stone by its full Stock ID, Caratlogic ID or certificate number, in any letter case. It also matches whole words in other stone details, such as a shape name.

  • To find several stones at once, use Paste IDs… in the column filters or Find by ID in Advanced Search.
  • If nothing matches, you'll see No diamonds match your search (or gems).
⚠ Part of an ID no longer finds a stone. Searching PO1147479 finds that stone, but PO1147 now finds nothing. Type the full ID, or paste a list of full IDs into the Stock ID column filter.

Changed

Carat, Reserved, Sold and Supplier Filters

  • Available Carat filters on available carats only. Before, the carat filter also counted reserved carats. To include them, filter on Total Carat (available + reserved), or choose Weigh by: Total in Advanced Search.
  • Reserved and Sold are now filtered in carats, not number of stones.
  • Supplier is now a multi-select showing supplier names and counts. It matches the stone's supplier, whether it came from a purchase order, a memo in, or was set directly on the stone. Only suppliers with stock are listed.
  • New for diamonds: filter by Origin Country and pick several Fancy Colors at once.
  • A minimum of 0 now works in number filters. Before, it was ignored.

💡
Good to Know

How Filters Work Together

  • Different filters narrow each other down. Shape: Round and Total Carat: 1–2 shows only round stones from 1 to 2 ct. Several values in one filter widen it: Round, Oval shows both.
  • Ranges include both ends. 1–2 ct includes stones of exactly 1 ct and 2 ct.
  • Counts show your whole inventory list. The number next to each option doesn't change as you add other filters.
  • Export All exports only the stones that match your filters and search. Export Selected and bulk actions use the rows you've ticked.
  • Filters stay while you work. They're kept when you open a stone and come back. Reloading the page or switching to Archive clears them, so save searches you'll need again.
  • On diamonds, switching between Natural and Lab-Grown keeps your filters, except Growth type, which only applies to Lab-Grown.
  • Column filters, Advanced Search and saved searches are on Gem Inventory and Diamond Inventory. Archive pages don't have them.
