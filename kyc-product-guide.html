Section 0

KYC basics & getting started

CaratLogic KYC is a general tool for collecting and verifying information. You design the forms yourself, so the same process works for any check. You send a form, the other person fills it in, and your team reviews it.

0.1

One product, many kinds of checks

Each check is simply a form you build in Forms Management. Create one form per check, name it clearly, and assign whichever form fits the person you are checking.

CheckWho it's forWhat the form might collect
KYC — Know Your CustomerCustomers and buyersCompany registration, tax and trade details, owners, ID and address proof
KYS — Know Your SupplierSuppliers and manufacturersBusiness licences, certifications, sourcing and compliance declarations, bank details
KYE — Know Your EmployeeEmployees and new hiresID documents, address proof, qualifications, emergency contacts, signed policies
Vendors, partners, brokersAnyone else you work withAgreements, insurance certificates, documents with expiry dates
Your own checkAnyoneWhatever questions and documents you need
Everything in this guide (assigning forms, statuses, reviews, reminders and emails) works the same way whatever the form is for. Screens use the word "KYC" throughout, but read it as "the check you sent".
0.2

How a request works

Every request follows the same path, whether it is KYC, KYS, KYE or your own check:

  1. You add a business contact on the Dashboard: a supplier, customer, partner, broker or vendor.
  2. You assign a form (for example your KYC, KYS or KYE form) to the contact. They receive an email with a secure verification link.
  3. The contact verifies their email with a one-time code (OTP), then fills in the form and submits it.
  4. Your team gets an alert in the notification bell, and by email if you have set that up.
  5. A reviewer checks the submission and chooses Approve, Conditionally Approve, Request Information or Reject. The contact is emailed the result.
  6. Reminders run automatically. Contacts who haven't finished are reminded. Approved contacts are asked to renew their KYC when the renewal period is up.
0.3

First-time setup checklist

If you are an administrator setting up KYC for the first time, do these steps in order before sending your first form:

  1. Connect your email server in Settings › Email Setup, so invitations are sent from your company's address. See 5.2
  2. Add the people who should be alerted about new submissions in Settings › KYC Submission Notifications. See 5.3
  3. Check the reminder settings. The defaults are weekly reminders and yearly renewal. See 5.1
  4. Prepare your forms in Forms Management: one for each check you run, such as KYC for customers and KYS for suppliers. Publish them and set the one you use most as the default. See Section 4
  5. Adjust the email wording in Email Template Configuration if you want to. See Section 6
  6. Add a contact and assign the form. See 1.4 and 1.5
Can't see a menu item, or are its buttons greyed out? Access is set per area (Dashboard, KYC Forms, Settings, Email Template Configuration) as No access, View only or Full access. Ask your administrator to change it.

Section 1

Dashboard

The Dashboard is where you spend most of your time. It lists all your business contacts with their KYC status, and it is where you add contacts, send forms and open reviews.

1.1

Dashboard overview and status summary

Two areas make up the Dashboard. The status cards at the top show how many contacts are in each KYC status. The contact list below shows each contact's ID, company, contact details, contact type and actions.

  • Total Contacts shows everyone in your list. The other cards count one status each, from Not Required through to Expired.
  • Click a status card to show only the contacts in that status. This is the quickest way to find work, for example everything that is Submitted.
  • Click a Contact ID to open that contact's details.
  • The top bar holds the product switcher, light/dark theme toggle, notification bell and your profile menu.
1.2

Search and filters

Use the search bar and filters to find contacts quickly.

  • Search by contact name, company or email.
  • KYC Status filter: select one or more statuses. For example, choose Submitted and Resubmitted together to see everything waiting for review.
  • Contact Types filter: show only suppliers, customers, partners, brokers or vendors.
  • Reset all clears the search and every filter.
1.3

Notifications

The bell icon in the top bar shows a count of unread notifications. When a contact submits their KYC, a KYC review required notification appears with the company and contact name.

  • Click a notification to go to the review.
  • Mark notifications as read or unread one at a time, mark them all as read, or clear the list.
  • Read status is personal. Marking something as read doesn't change your colleagues' view.
1.4

Add a business contact

Before you can send a KYC form, the company must exist as a business contact.

  1. Click + Add Business Contact at the top right of the Dashboard.
  2. Fill in the company, address, tax, currency and contact person details. Fields marked as required must be filled before you can save.
  3. Choose the contact type: Supplier, Customer, Partner, Broker or Vendor. A contact can have more than one type. Customer is selected by default.
  4. Enter a primary email. This is where the KYC invitation goes, and the contact uses it to verify themselves.
  5. Save. The new contact appears in the list with status Not Required.
Adding a contact doesn't send them anything. Nothing reaches the contact until you assign a form.
1.5

Contact actions and assigning a form

Click the ··· menu at the end of a contact's row to see what you can do with that contact:

  • View: open the contact's details.
  • Edit: update the contact's details.
  • Fill KYC: fill in and submit the form yourself on the contact's behalf, for example when they send the details by phone or on paper. It counts as a normal submission, so the usual emails and notifications are sent.
  • Review KYC: open the contact's latest submission.
  • Assign Form: send a KYC form to this contact.
  • Delete: permanently remove the contact. You will be asked to confirm, and this can't be undone.
Once a contact is Approved, their KYC is locked. Fill KYC and Assign Form are not available for approved contacts.
1.6

Select several contacts at once

To send the same form to many contacts, tick the checkbox beside each one. A count of selected contacts appears below the table. You can then run one action for all of them from the Operations menu.

1.7

Assign a form to several contacts

With your contacts selected, open Operations and choose Assign Form. The number next to it tells you how many contacts will receive the form.

  • You can send to up to 100 contacts at a time.
  • You get a result for each contact, so you can see which invitations went out and which need another attempt.
1.8

The Assign KYC Form popup

The popup is the same whether you assign to one contact or to many. Every selected contact gets the same form and version, and each contact gets their own personal verification link.

  1. Select form and Version. Only published, active versions appear in the list. Or tick Use the default form instead.
  2. Keep Notify customer via email ticked to email the invitation to the contact.
  3. Check the recipients list.
  4. Click Assign Form & Send. The contact's status changes to In Progress.

What the contact receives

  • A verification link that works for 30 days.
  • After they verify their email, 24 hours of form access. If that runs out, they verify again with a new code using the same link.
Sending the same form and version again to a contact who is still In Progress keeps their saved answers. Sending a different form or version, or re-sending after a KYC has been closed, starts a fresh KYC round. Earlier answers are prefilled where possible.
Section 2

What your contact sees

Knowing your contact's side of the process helps you answer their questions. Contacts don't need a CaratLogic account. They only need access to the email address you have on file for them.

2.1

Verifying and filling in the form

  1. The contact opens the invitation email and clicks Start Verification.
  2. They enter their email address, which must match the primary email on their contact record, and request a one-time code.
  3. A 6-digit code arrives by email. It is valid for 15 minutes. They can request a new one after 60 seconds.
  4. After entering the code, they fill in the form page by page with Previous and Next. Each page is saved when they click Next, so they can stop and come back later.
  5. On the last page they click Submit. Your team is alerted, and the contact receives a confirmation email.
After 5 wrong codes the link stops working, and you need to send the contact a new invitation. If a contact says their link has expired, assign the form again. They will get a fresh 30-day link.
2.2

When you ask for more information

If a reviewer chooses Request Information, the contact receives an email with your comment and an Update Submission button. This link is valid for 7 days. The contact verifies again, edits their answers and resubmits, and the status changes to Resubmitted.

A Conditionally Approved email also includes a Resubmit KYC button, so the contact can respond to your conditions.

Section 3

Review Submissions

Review Submissions is your compliance team's work queue. Here you find submitted KYCs, check the answers and documents, and record a decision.

3.1

Find a submission

The list shows each submission's company, contact details, contact types, KYC form and version, submission date and status.

  • Search by contact name, company, email, phone number or Contact ID.
  • Click Review on a row to open the submission.
3.2

Check the submission details

The top of the review screen shows the company, the current status, which form was used, when it was submitted and how many pages were completed. Below that, each section of the form can be expanded to show the contact's answers and uploaded documents.

Tip: open every section and look at each uploaded document before you decide. Documents can be downloaded from this screen.
3.3

Submission history

Every KYC round is kept, including earlier years and resubmissions. Click History to see all of a contact's submissions, newest first. The Current label marks the active one, so you can tell it apart from older rounds or older form versions.

3.4

Record your decision

When you've finished checking, open Review Actions and choose one of the following:

ActionUse it whenThe contact receives
ApproveEverything is correct and complete.An Approved email. The KYC is now locked.
Conditionally ApproveYou can accept it now, but something still needs to be followed up.An email with your notes and a Resubmit KYC button.
Request InformationDetails or documents are missing or unclear.An email with your notes and a 7-day Update Submission link.
RejectThe KYC can't be accepted.A Rejected email with your notes.

For every action except Approve, a box appears where you can add a note. The note is included in the email, so write it for the contact.

Review Actions only appear when a submission is waiting for a decision (Submitted, Under Review, Info Required or Resubmitted), and only for users with full Dashboard access.
Section 4

Forms Management

Forms Management is where you decide what the product checks. Build a separate form for each purpose, such as a KYC form for customers, a KYS form for suppliers and a KYE form for employees. Every form keeps a full version history.

4.1

How form versions work

If you understand this, the rest of Forms Management is straightforward:

  • Draft: a version you are still editing. Contacts can't receive a draft. Each form can have only one draft at a time.
  • Published: a finished version that can be sent to contacts. Published versions can't be changed. To change one, click Edit, which creates a new draft from it, and then publish that draft as the next version.
  • Default: the version that is preselected when you assign a form. Publishing a new version doesn't change the default automatically. Set it yourself.
This design keeps existing submissions safe. A contact's answers always stay linked to the exact version they filled in, even after you change the form.
4.2

The form list

The list shows every KYC form with its latest version, who created it and when it was last updated. Use the search bar to find a form by name. The Default label shows which form is used for new assignments.

4.3

Create a form

  1. Click Create Form.
  2. Enter a form name and, if you like, a description. Each form name must be unique, so name forms by purpose, for example "Supplier KYS" or "Employee KYE".
  3. A version 1 draft is created and opens in the form editor.
4.4

The form editor

The editor is where you build the form. Each page appears as a tab across the top, and the contact moves through the pages with Next and Previous.

  • Add page adds a new tab. Add Field adds a question to the current page.
  • Save changes saves your draft. Save often.
  • Publish makes this version available to send. It is enabled once the draft has been saved.
4.5

Form menu: rename or reset

The menu next to Publish has two options:

  • Rename form: change the form's title.
  • Reset form to default: replace the draft with the standard starting layout.
Reset replaces everything in the current draft. Only use it if you want to start over.
4.6

Page menu: rename, clear or delete

Each page tab has its own menu with Rename page, Clear all fields and Delete page. Options that don't apply are greyed out. For example, you can't delete the last page, because every form needs at least one.

4.7

Add and set up fields

Click Add Field and fill in the Label (the question), Field Type, an optional Placeholder (example text shown in the box) and an optional Description (help text, which can be formatted).

Field typeUse it for
Text / Text AreaShort answers (up to 500 characters) or longer answers (up to 5,000 characters)
Email / Phone / NumberAnswers that are checked for the right format
Date / TimeDates can be limited to past-only or future-only
Dropdown / Radio Group / Multi SelectChoosing from options you list. Multi Select allows more than one choice.
Checkbox / ToggleYes/no answers and declarations
ImagePhotos: JPEG, PNG, GIF or WebP, up to 5 MB
FileDocuments: PDF, Word or Excel, up to 10 MB
Docs with ExpiryDocuments that expire, such as passports or licences. The contact also enters the expiry date, and automatic reminders are sent before it passes.

Field switches

  • Required: the contact must answer this field before they can move on.
  • Visible: whether the field is shown on the form.
  • Editable: whether the contact can change the value. Turn it off for values you fill in for them.
4.8

Manage fields and save your draft

Each field appears as a card. On the card you can switch Required on or off, show or hide the field, edit it, or delete it.

  • Save changes stores your edits in the draft.
  • Discard removes any edits you haven't saved yet.
  • Publish makes this version ready to send.
4.9

Version history

Click a form in the list to expand it and see all of its published versions with their version numbers and publish dates. The Latest label marks the newest version, and the Default label marks the one used for new assignments. They are not always the same version.

4.10

Version actions

Each version has its own menu:

  • Preview: see the form exactly as a contact would.
  • Edit: start a new draft based on this version. The published version itself doesn't change.
  • Duplicate: copy this version into a brand-new form. This is useful for building, say, a supplier form from your customer form.
  • Set as default: use this version for new assignments.
  • Deactivate: stop the version being sent to new contacts. Contacts who are already filling it in can still submit. You can reactivate it later. You can't deactivate the default version, so set a different default first.
4.11

Preview a form

Preview shows the form as your contact will see it. Nothing you type in preview is saved. Before you send a new version, use it to check the page order, labels, required markers (*) and the Previous/Next buttons.

Section 5

Settings

Settings control automatic reminders, how often KYC must be renewed, which email server sends your emails, and who on your team is told about new submissions.

5.1

Reminder settings

  • KYC Mail Frequency: how often reminders are sent. The options are Weekly (default), Biweekly and Monthly.
  • KYC Renewal Interval: how long an approval lasts before the contact is asked to renew. The options are 30, 90, 180, 365 (default) or 730 days.
  • Auto-Reminders: turns all automatic reminder emails on or off.
  • Reset to defaults restores Weekly and 365 days.

What Auto-Reminders sends

  • Unfinished KYC: contacts who are In Progress or Info Required get a reminder at your chosen frequency, with a fresh 30-day link.
  • Renewal: Approved and Conditionally Approved contacts get a renewal reminder once their renewal interval has passed.
  • Expiring documents: contacts whose Docs with Expiry uploads expire within 30 days, or have already expired, are asked to upload new ones.
Turning Auto-Reminders off stops all three kinds of reminder, including document expiry reminders.
5.2

Email setup

Connect KYC to your company's email server (SMTP) so invitations and notifications come from your own address. You need these details from your IT team or email provider:

  • SMTP server and port
  • Username and password
  • Sender email and sender name: the address and name your contacts see in their inbox.
5.3

Submission notification recipients

List the colleagues who should get an email when a contact submits their KYC. The email names the contact and company, and it links straight to the review screen.

  • Add, edit or remove addresses at any time.
  • Everyone on the list receives the same alert.
  • The list starts empty, so no submission emails are sent until you add at least one address.

6.1

6.2

Edit a template and preview it

Each template has four parts you can edit:

  • Greeting, such as "Hello". The contact's name is added after it automatically.
  • Summary Heading: the large title at the top of the email.
  • Message and Notes: the body text, with bold, italic, headings, lists, colours and highlights.

You don't need to type placeholders. The company name, dates, reviewer comments and button are added automatically, and the Email Preview on the right shows the finished email. Click Save to keep your changes, or Reset to Defaults to go back to the original wording.

Section 7

Common questions

Can I use this for things other than customer KYC?

Yes. Build a form for any check, such as KYS for suppliers, KYE for employees, or vendor and partner onboarding. Then assign it to the right contact. Choose the matching contact type when you add the person, so you can filter by it later.

My contact says their link has expired.

Invitation links last 30 days, and Request Information links last 7 days. Assign the form again to send a new link. If it is the same form and version, their saved answers are kept.

My contact isn't receiving the code.

The code goes to the primary email on the contact record, so check that it is correct. Ask them to check their spam folder. If none of your emails arrive, check Email Setup.

I need to change a form that has already been sent.

Click Edit on the version, make your changes, and publish them as a new version. Then choose Set as default. Contacts who already have the old version can finish it as normal.

Why can't I assign a form to this contact?

Check three things: the contact might already be Approved, which locks their KYC; they might not have a primary email; or the form or version might be deactivated.

Nobody on my team was told about a new submission.

The notification bell always shows new submissions. Email alerts are only sent to addresses on the KYC Submission Notification list.

The buttons on a screen are greyed out.

You probably have view-only access to that area. Ask your administrator for full access.
