Brokerage Management & Polish Zloty — Release Notes

This release lets you track the brokerage you owe your brokers. Give each broker a default rate and a bank account, create a brokerage debit note with an invoice or later from Transactions, and the debit note stays in step when the invoice changes. Polish Zloty (PLN) is also now available as a currency.

October 20, 2026

New

Brokerage Rate on Broker Contacts

Where: Business Contacts → Add Business Contact or Edit Business Contact

When a contact's Contact Type includes Broker, the Broker field is replaced by Brokerage (%). This is the broker's default rate for their debit notes, and it can be changed on each debit note.

  • Enter a rate from 0 to 100, up to two decimals. Leaving it empty saves 0%.
  • A broker needs at least one bank account, since brokerage is paid into it. Adding the Broker type starts an empty bank account for you to fill in.
  • Adding the Broker type clears any broker assigned to the contact, because a broker can't have a broker of their own.
  • The contact's details page shows Brokerage (%) for brokers.
⚠ Broker type is locked once saved. After a contact is saved as a broker, Broker can't be removed from its contact types. The chip is greyed out and shows Broker can't be removed once a contact is saved as a broker. Other contact types can still be changed.

Create a Brokerage Debit Note With an Invoice

Where: Create Invoice → Invoice Details → Create brokerage debit note

When the invoice's customer has a broker, a Create brokerage debit note block appears under the Invoice Summary. It is switched on when the broker has a bank account. The invoice and its debit note are saved together.

What you fill in

  • Broker is filled in from the customer and can't be changed here.
  • Brokerage (%) starts at the broker's default, e.g. Broker's default is 2.00%.
  • Calculated on is the subtotal less discount, before tax.
  • Pay into lists the broker's bank accounts. The account in the invoice currency is picked first.
  • Debit Note Template sets the PDF layout.
  • Brokerage payable updates as you change the items, discount or rate.

Review & confirm shows a Brokerage debit note card with the broker, rate, account and amount. After saving you'll see e.g. Debit note DBN-26-0007 created.

Good to know

  • If the broker has no bank account, an amber notice says so, with an Add bank account button that opens the broker in a new tab. When you come back, the block picks up the new account.
  • Switch it off to create the invoice without a debit note. You can add one later from Transactions → Debit Notes.
  • Proforma invoices don't have this block. It's offered when a proforma is converted to an invoice.
  • If the debit note can't be created, the invoice isn't saved either, so you never end up with one without the other.

Create a Debit Note for an Existing Invoice

Where: Transactions → Debit Notes → Create Debit Note, or an invoice's details page → actions menu → Create debit note

Create a brokerage debit note for an invoice that was saved without one. Opening it from an invoice picks that invoice for you.

StepWhat it does
1. InvoiceLists final invoices that have a broker. Shows the customer, status, invoice date, total and items.
2. BrokerShows the broker's details. Set Brokerage (%) and the Pay into account.
3. Debit Note PDF TemplateChoose the PDF layout.
4. Brokerage SummaryInvoice subtotal, discount, calculated on, brokerage and Brokerage payable.

Click Create Debit Note. The Create debit note? dialog shows the amount and the broker's contact name, email, mobile, tax ID, address and Pay into account before you confirm.

⚠ One active debit note per invoice. If the invoice already has one, you'll see Debit note already exists with a link to it, e.g. View DBN-26-0007. Cancel it first to create a new one. On the invoice's details page, the menu item reads View debit note DBN-26-0007 instead.

A new tab lists every brokerage debit note, newest first.

Debit Note Details and PDF

Where: Transactions → Debit Notes → open a debit note

The details page leads with Brokerage payable, e.g. 2.00% of $9,500.00 on invoice INV-26-0142, followed by the created date, invoice, customer, rate and currency.

  • The Broker card shows the broker's email, phone, GSTIN or VAT number, address and the Pay into account, with a View contact link.
  • The Breakdown card shows invoice subtotal, discount, calculated on and brokerage.
  • Download, print, view or share the PDF from the page header and actions menu.

What the PDF shows

  • Debit note number and date, the reference invoice and its date, and the customer.
  • The broker's name, address and GSTIN in the To: block.
  • One line, e.g. Brokerage on invoice INV-26-0142 to Luxury Jewelers Inc., with Calculated On, Rate and Amount.
  • A Pay Into block with the broker's bank, account name, account number, IFSC or SWIFT, and currency.
  • Brokerage Payable as the total.

🔄
New

Debit Notes Follow the Invoice

How brokerage is worked out

Brokerage is the rate applied to the invoice subtotal less discount. Tax and shipping are not included. For example, a $10,000.00 subtotal with a 5% discount is calculated on $9,500.00, and at 2.00% the brokerage payable is $190.00.

When the invoice changes

  • Editing the invoice recalculates its active debit note at the same rate. A new debit note PDF is created only when the amount changes, so a tax or shipping change leaves it alone.
  • Cancelling the invoice cancels its active debit note too.

Cancelling a debit note

Choose Cancel Debit Note from the list or the details page. The debit note stays on record as Cancelled, and you can then create a new one for the same invoice.

Debit notes are in your base currency, the same as the invoice's stored amounts.

Polish Zloty (PLN)

Polish Zloty (PLN, symbol zł) is now available wherever you choose a currency.

  • Invoice, Quotation, Memo Out and Credit Note currency, and the conversion currency shown on the invoice PDF.
  • A business contact's currency, and the currency of their bank accounts.
  • The payment currency on Purchase Management and Memo In.
  • PLN amounts show with the zł symbol in the app and in emails. PDFs show the currency code, e.g. PLN 1,250.00.
  • Converted amounts now round half-cents the same way in the app and on PDFs, for PLN and every other currency.
⚠ Exchange rate. PLN isn't in Exchange Rate Configuration yet, so its rate is taken from live market rates. You can still type your own rate on the document. PLN can't be chosen as your company's base currency.
