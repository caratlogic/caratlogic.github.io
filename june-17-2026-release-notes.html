Release Notes

Automatic partner stone creation, safer inventory deletion, improved carat editing, barcode quantity labels, and clearer inventory allocation visibility.

June 17, 2026
01
🤝
New

Automatic Partner Stone Record Creation

Partner stone records are now created automatically when adding gems or diamonds with Partner Stone selected as the ownership type.

  • A partner stone record is automatically created when creating a gem or diamond with Partner Stone ownership
  • Newly created records appear immediately in the Partner Management module
  • Partner details, contributions, and profit-sharing information can be added later
  • Eliminates the need to manually create partnership records after inventory creation
Partnership inventory is now tracked from the moment it is created, ensuring no records are missed.
02
🛡
Enhanced

Safer Inventory Deletion

Inventory deletion rules have been strengthened to prevent accidental removal of gems and diamonds involved in transactions.

  • Gems and diamonds can only be deleted when their entire carat quantity is still available
  • Deletion is blocked if any portion of the inventory has been sold or reserved
  • Clear validation messages explain why deletion is not permitted
  • Inventory can still be archived when permanent deletion is not allowed
This ensures historical sales and reservation data remain protected and consistent.
03
💎
Enhanced

Zero Available Carats Support During Editing

Gem and diamond inventory can now be updated with 0 Available Carats when editing existing records.

  • Available Carats can be set to zero for fully sold or fully reserved inventory
  • Inventory status is automatically updated based on availability, including Sold, Memo Out, and Out of Stock
  • Items are archived automatically when applicable
  • Minimum carat validation continues to apply when creating new inventory records
This provides better support for inventory that has been completely allocated while preserving accurate stock status.
04
🏷
Enhanced

In-Stock Quantity on Barcode Labels

Barcode labels can now display the available piece quantity alongside carat weight.

  • Added support for displaying In Stock Quantity on gem and diamond barcode labels
  • Can be enabled independently from Settings → Barcode
  • Displays the number of available pieces directly on printed labels
  • Particularly useful for parcels, sets, and pairs where quantity is an important identifier
Barcode labels now provide more complete inventory information at a glance.
05
✏
Enhanced

Clearer Available Carats Labeling

The carat field on gem and diamond edit forms has been renamed to improve clarity during inventory updates.

  • Total Weight has been renamed to Available Carats on edit screens
  • Makes it clear that users are editing the currently available quantity
  • Helps distinguish available stock from the historical total carat amount
  • Supported across both gem and diamond inventory forms
The updated labeling reduces confusion and provides a more intuitive editing experience.
06
📊
Enhanced

Carat Summary During Inventory Editing

Edit forms now provide a quick summary of carat distribution to give better visibility into inventory usage.

  • Displays Sold Carats, Reserved Carats, and Total Carats directly below the Available Carats field
  • Provides a complete view of inventory allocation while editing
  • Eliminates the need to navigate to other screens to verify stock usage
  • Available across both gem and diamond inventory
Users can now understand inventory availability and allocation at a glance while making updates.
