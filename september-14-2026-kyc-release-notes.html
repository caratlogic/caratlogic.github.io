KYC — Release Notes

Introducing CaratLogic KYC, a new product for collecting and reviewing Know Your Customer information from your business contacts. This release adds a notification centre, submission email alerts, bulk invitations, repeat and annual KYC cycles, and role-based access.

September 14, 2026
01
🔔
New

Notification Centre in the Dashboard

The bell in the top bar shows an unread count and opens a panel listing recent activity.

  • A KYC review required notification appears every time a business contact submits their KYC
  • Each notification names the company and the contact who submitted
  • Mark items read or unread one at a time, or clear the whole list in a single action
  • Read state is tracked per user, so marking something read doesn't change the list for colleagues
Submissions surface in the dashboard as they arrive, with each reviewer keeping their own view of what's been read.
02
📧
New

Email Alerts When a Contact Submits

Nominate the colleagues who should be emailed the moment a contact submits their KYC.

  • A new settings page to add, edit and remove recipient addresses
  • Everyone on the list receives the same alert
  • The email names the contact, their company and the submission status, and links straight to the review screen
  • Separate from the confirmation email the contact receives
  • No alerts are sent until you add the first recipient
Reviewers hear about a submission without having to watch the dashboard.
03
📩
New

Bulk KYC Invitations

Select several business contacts from the list and send KYC invitations to all of them in one action.

  • Up to 100 contacts per send
  • Choose which form and version to send, exactly as with a single invitation
  • Each contact is reported back individually, so you can see which invitations went out and which need another attempt
Onboarding a whole book of contacts no longer means sending invitations one at a time.
04
🔗
Enhanced

Links That Last

Verification links now stay valid for 30 days instead of 30 minutes.

  • Applies to both invitation emails and reminder emails
  • A contact who doesn't reach the form straight away no longer has to ask for a fresh link
Fewer expired-link requests, and fewer invitations that have to be resent.
05
📜
New

Full Submission History

A business contact can now complete KYC more than once on the same form, and every submission is kept.

  • Previously the latest record replaced what came before
  • You can now look back at what a contact submitted in earlier rounds
Repeat and annual KYC now build a record over time instead of overwriting it.
06
🔄
New

New Cycles Start on Their Own

Once a KYC is closed — submitted, approved, conditionally approved, rejected or expired — the next time the contact opens their portal they begin a fresh cycle.

  • The new cycle is prefilled with their previous answers, so they only need to update what has changed
  • A KYC that is still in progress simply carries on where it left off, exactly as before
Annual renewals start themselves, with no re-keying of information that hasn't changed.
07
📋
New

Submissions List for Reviewers

A paginated list of submissions for working through a review queue.

  • Narrow to a single contact, a particular form, or one or more KYC statuses
  • Show only those that have actually been submitted
  • Doubles as the submission history for an individual contact
One screen for both the review queue and a contact's past submissions.
08
🔍
Enhanced

Filter by Several Statuses at Once

The contact list now accepts more than one KYC status in the filter.

  • Combine statuses such as Submitted and Resubmitted in a single view
  • Replaces filtering by one status at a time
Everything awaiting attention can be seen in one list.
09
🔐
New

Role-Based Access

Access is now controlled per area: Dashboard, KYC Forms, Settings, and Email Template Configuration.

  • Each area can be set to no access, view only, or full access
  • Users without access don't see the screen at all
  • Users with view-only access can open the screen, but its controls are disabled
Sensitive KYC data and configuration can be limited to the people who need them.
10
🔄
New

Product Switcher

A switcher in the top bar for moving between CaratLogic products without signing in again.

KYC sits alongside your other CaratLogic products, one sign-in away.
11
⚙
Enhanced

Settings Split into Dedicated Pages

Reminder settings and email setup are now separate pages rather than tabs.

  • Each sits alongside the new submission alert recipients page
Settings are easier to find now that each area has its own page.
12
✨
Enhanced

Smaller Improvements

  • Light and dark theme toggle on the public KYC form, so contacts can pick either
  • The business contact add and edit screens were reorganised, with consistent country and state selectors
  • Refreshed branding and logo across the platform
  • Fixed downloading documents from the KYC review screen
  • Fixed the footer on the public KYC form
  • The submission success screen now returns the contact to the home page
