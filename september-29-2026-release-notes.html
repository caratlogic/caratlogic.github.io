Inventory & Transactions — Release Notes

This release adds Excel export for invoices, proformas, quotations and memo outs, lets you choose which columns your clients see, extends Excel line item upload to every sales document, pushes lab-grown diamonds to VDB automatically, links certificate numbers to lab verification pages, simplifies diamond color requirements, renames intensity settings, and cleans up media when items are permanently deleted.

September 28–29, 2026

☑
New

Choose What Goes into Product Details

Where: Template Configurations → Excel Exports

You can now pick which columns your clients see in the new Product details (.xlsx) export, separately for gems and diamonds. Your choice applies to every invoice, proforma, quotation and memo out.

  • Required columns are always exported and shown as locked.
    Gems: Stock Prefix, Stock Sequence, Gem Type, Total Weight.
    Diamonds: Stock Prefix, Stock Sequence, Stock Type, Shape, Weight, Clarity, Color, Fancy Color.
  • Cost Price Per Carat is off by default, so cost prices never reach a client unless you turn them on.
  • Your additional fields can be exported too, and they're on by default.
  • You can search for a column, or show or hide a whole section at once. These settings don't affect the Line items file.
⚠ Every column left on here is visible to your client. If you turn on Cost Price Per Carat, your cost prices go to every client who receives a Product details file. Internal columns such as Price Code, Discount %, Status, Treatment Notes and your additional fields are exported too when they're on. Review both the Gem and Diamond tabs and turn off anything your clients shouldn't see before you send your first file.

New

Export a Document to Excel

Where: Document details → Download, or the row menu in any list → PDF

Every invoice, proforma, quotation and memo out can now be downloaded as two Excel files, next to its PDF.

  • Line items (.xlsx) uses the same columns as the upload template, services included. You can download it from one document and upload it into another.
  • Product details (.xlsx) is made for sending to clients. It has one sheet for gems and one for diamonds.
  • In Product details, each stone's details come first, followed by Currency, Selling Price Per Carat, Total Price (exc. tax), the tax columns (VAT, SGST, CGST, IGST, UTGST) and Total Price (inc. tax). Each Stock ID links to the stone's page.
  • Download options are now named after the document and file type, e.g. Invoice (.pdf). Quotation rows now have the same PDF menu as invoices.
⚠ Check your columns before sending Product details to a client. The file contains every column turned on in Template Configurations → Excel Exports. If Cost Price Per Carat or other internal fields (such as Price Code, Discount % or your additional fields) are turned on, your client will see them. Set up the columns first — see the section above.

Upload Line Items to Any Sales Document

Where: Transactions or Memo Out → Upload items

Excel upload now works on invoices, proformas, quotations and memo outs. Fill in the template, upload it, check the rows, and add them in one step.

  • Upload items is on the list pages and on the create pages. On a create page, select a customer first.
  • Download the template (add_line_items_template.xlsx). For stones, Item Type (gem or diamond) and Stock ID are required. Description, Quantity, Carats, Selling Price Per Carat and Total Price are optional. An empty cell uses the stone's own value.
  • Services. Set Item Type to service and fill in a Description and a Total Price. It's added alongside your stones.
  • Rows without a price are kept. If neither the sheet nor the stone has a price, the row is added at 0 with a “No price set” warning, and you can set the price on the next page.
  • Split parcels and sets. To sell a parcel in groups, put its Stock ID on several rows. The rows are checked together against the pieces and carats left. If they add up to more, the message shows under the Quantity or Carats cell that's over.
  • Your sheet's price always wins, whether the page is showing cost or selling prices. If you give only a Total Price, the price per carat is calculated from it.
  • Fix rows before adding. You can edit any cell in the review table, and each problem shows under the cell it belongs to. If you click Add with unsaved edits, you're asked whether to save them first.
  • Rows are rejected if the stone is not found, is archived, or is not In Stock or on Memo Out.
  • Up to 500 lines per document (was 100). Bulk actions from the gem and diamond lists get the same limit.

Send Your Lab-Grown Diamonds to VDB Automatically

Where: App Integrations → VDB Config (admins only)

CaratLogic can now upload your inventory to VDB's SFTP server on a schedule, so your VDB listings stay up to date without manual uploads.

Set it up once

  • Enter VDB's SFTP details: host, port, username, and a password or private key.
  • Choose the remote directory and how often to push: every 1, 6, 12 or 24 hours.
  • Pick the inventory to send. Each goes as its own CSV file, and you can give each one a custom file path.
  • When you save, CaratLogic tests the connection and checks the folders exist, so a typo is caught straight away.
  • Passwords and keys are stored encrypted and never shown again. To keep a saved one, leave the field blank.
  • To pause without losing your settings, turn off Push enabled.

What gets sent

  • Lab-grown diamonds that are In Stock (listed on VDB as On-hand) or on Memo Out (listed as Unavailable). Archived and deleted stones are left out.
  • The file uses VDB's own lab-grown template. It includes grading, measurements and proportions; selling price per carat and total price; up to 6 images and a video; certificate lab, number and link; and stone location, taken from the warehouse address.
  • Each file is uploaded under a temporary name and swapped in when complete, so VDB never picks up a half-written file.
Natural diamonds and gems can already be selected, but they will only be sent once VDB's templates for them are added.

Push now

Admins can send an update right away from Diamond Inventory → More Actions → Push to VDB. It runs in the background while you keep working.

Push logs

The Push logs tab on VDB Config shows every push:

  • when it ran
  • whether it was scheduled or manual, and who started it
  • its result: Success, Partial, Failed or Interrupted
  • for each file, the rows uploaded or the error
  • Summary cards show the last push, the next scheduled push, your success rate, and how many pushes failed or partly failed.
  • Filter by status, inventory or date range. History is kept for a year.
  • If a scheduled push fails, it is retried within the hour instead of waiting a full interval.

Certificate Numbers Link to the Lab's Verification Page

Certificate numbers for GIA, GCAL and IGI now open the lab's report-check page in a new tab.

  • Works in the inventory list and on the detail page, for both gems and diamonds
  • Diamonds already had IGI links. Gems now have them too.
  • GCAL has been added to the list of certificate labs

💎
Changed

Diamonds: Color Grade or Fancy Color, Not Both

A diamond now needs only one of Color Grade (D–Z) or Fancy Color. Before, a Color Grade was always required, even for fancy-colored stones.

  • Applies to the add/edit form, bulk import and the API
  • The bulk import template always includes both the Color and Fancy Color columns. Each row needs a value in at least one of them.
07
⚙
Changed

Settings Renamed

  • Gem settings: Color Intensities is now Color Intensity
  • Diamond settings: Fancy Intensities is now Fancy Intensity
  • In the diamond inventory table, the Color Intensity column is now Fancy Color Intensity. If you had renamed this column yourself, your name is kept.
08
🗑
Changed

Media Is Removed When an Item Is Permanently Deleted

When a gem or diamond is permanently (hard) deleted, its images, videos, certificate files and uploaded custom-field files are also deleted from storage.

Files linked from outside CaratLogic are never touched. If the file cleanup fails, the item is still deleted.

Other Improvements

  • Export all gems or diamonds now works for any inventory size. Before, exports of more than 1,000 stones failed. When an export does fail, the message now says why.
  • Line tables on review and details pages now show about five rows and scroll, with the header and total kept in view.
  • Quotation and memo out summaries now show a single total.
