Two-Step Verification & Add by IGI Certificate — Release Notes

This release adds two-step verification to your CaratLogic sign-in. Protect your account with a code from an authenticator app, keep backup codes for a lost phone, and, as an admin, require it for everyone in your organization. You can also add a diamond or gem from its IGI report number, with the form filled in from the report and the certificate PDF attached.

November 17, 2026
01
🔐
New

Two-Step Verification

Where: Sidebar → Security

Two-step verification adds a second check when you sign in. After your password, you enter a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone. The app shows a new code every 30 seconds, so someone who learns your password still can't sign in without your phone.

  • It protects your sign-in to every CaratLogic application, since they all use the same account.
  • Every user can turn it on for their own account. It is off by default.
  • Admins can require it for the whole organization (see 05).
  • Use any free authenticator app. Google Authenticator and Microsoft Authenticator are linked on the setup screen. Authy, 1Password and other authenticator apps work too.

Set Up Two-Step Verification

Where: Security → Your Two-Step Verification → Set up

Setup takes about a minute and is written for people who have never used an authenticator app.

StepWhat you do
1. Install an appInstall a free authenticator app on your phone. iPhone and Android store links are shown for Google Authenticator and Microsoft Authenticator.
2. Scan the QR codeIn the app, tap + or Add account, choose Scan a QR code and point your camera at the screen. Can't scan it? Copy the setup key shown next to the QR code into the app instead. Setting up on your phone? Tap Open in authenticator app.
3. Enter the codeClick Next: enter the code, type the 6-digit code the app shows for CaratLogic, and click Verify and turn on.
4. Save your backup codesYou get 10 backup codes, e.g. ABCDE-FGHIJ. Copy them or click Download .txt, tick I've saved these codes somewhere safe, then click Done.
  • Each step has a Having trouble? section that answers the common problems, such as a QR code the app can't read or a code that isn't accepted.
  • Two-step verification is only turned on once a code from the new app is accepted, so a scan that didn't work never locks you out.
  • You'll get an email confirming that two-step verification is on.
⚠ Backup codes are shown only once. Each code lets you sign in once without your phone. Keep them somewhere safe, away from your phone.

Signing In With Two-Step Verification

After you enter your username and password, a Two-step verification screen asks for the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. The code is checked as soon as you type the sixth digit.

  • Lost your phone? Click Use a backup code instead and enter one of your backup codes. Each backup code works once.
  • Back to login returns to the password screen.
  • Finish within 10 minutes of entering your password. After that you'll see Your sign-in timed out. Please log in again.
  • If a code isn't accepted, type the one showing right now, and check that your phone's date and time update automatically.
  • Resetting a forgotten password doesn't skip this step. After setting a new password, you sign in and enter your code as usual.
⚠ Too many incorrect codes. After 5 incorrect codes in a row, two-step verification is locked for 15 minutes and you'll see Too many incorrect codes. Try again in 15 minutes.

Require Two-Step Verification for Your Organization

Where: Security → Organization-Wide Two-Step Verification (admins only)

Turn on the switch to require two-step verification for every user in your organization, including admins. The Require two-step verification for all users? dialog tells you how many users haven't set it up yet before you confirm.

  • The card shows how many active users have set it up, e.g. 8 of 12 users enrolled, with a progress bar. View users opens the Users list so you can see who hasn't.
  • You don't need to set it up yourself first. Like everyone else, you'll be asked at your next sign-in.
  • Users who haven't set it up are asked to do so at their next sign-in (see 06).
  • While it's required, users can't disable their own two-step verification.
  • Turn the switch off to make it optional again. Users who already set it up keep it.
Existing sessions remain active. Users who are signed in when you turn it on are asked for a code when their session expires, within 6 hours.

Setup at Sign-In When It's Required

When your organization requires two-step verification and you haven't set it up, the screen after your password is Set up two-step verification, marked Required by your organization.

  • Follow the same steps as on the Security page: install an app, scan the QR code, then enter the code and click Verify and continue.
  • Save your backup codes on the Save your backup codes screen, then click Continue to finish signing in.
  • From then on, you sign in with your password and a code.

Two-Step Status and Reset on the Users List

Where: Role Management → Users (admins only)

  • A new Two-Step column shows each user's status: Enabled, Disabled, or Pending setup when your organization requires it.
  • A new row action, Reset two-step verification, helps a user who lost their phone. It's available for users who have it on. You can't reset your own; use the Security page instead.
  • The Reset two-step verification dialog explains that the user's authenticator app and backup codes will stop working. If your organization requires it, they set up a new app at their next sign-in. If not, they sign in with their password until they turn it on again.
  • The user is emailed to let them know.

Add a Diamond or Gem by IGI Certificate

Where: Diamond Inventory → Add Diamond → Add by certificate, or Gem Inventory → Add Gem → Add by certificate

The Add Diamond and Add Gem buttons now open a menu. Add manually opens the usual form. Add by certificate fetches the stone's details from its IGI report.

  • In the Add diamond by certificate or Add gem by certificate dialog, choose IGI as the Lab and enter the Report / certificate number, e.g. 161497209.
  • Report numbers are 4 to 30 letters, digits or dashes.
  • Click Continue. CaratLogic checks the report with IGI, then opens the Add Diamond or Add Gem form with the details filled in.
  • Natural diamonds, lab-grown diamonds and colored stones are supported.
Nothing is added to your inventory until you review the form and save it.

What's Filled In From the IGI Report

Only the fields the report includes are filled in. Grades are matched to the values and abbreviations in your settings, so the form uses your own wording.

StoneFields filled in
DiamondDiamond Type (Natural or Lab Grown, with growth type CVD or HPHT when the report states it), shape, carat weight, color grade or fancy color and intensity, clarity, cut, polish, symmetry, fluorescence intensity and color, measurements, table %, depth %, crown height and angle, pavilion depth and angle, girdle, culet, origin and laser inscription
GemGem type (from the report's variety or species), carat weight, shape, color, color modifier, color description, origin and measurements
Certificate 1Certificate type IGI, the report number, the issue date, the report's comments and the IGI certificate PDF

How IGI wording is matched

  • ROUND BRILLIANT becomes Round, SQUARE MODIFIED BRILLIANT becomes Princess, and EMERALD CUT becomes Emerald.
  • IGI's IDEAL cut and EXC polish or symmetry match your Ideal and Excellent values.
  • FANCY INTENSE YELLOW becomes fancy color Yellow with intensity Fancy Intense.
  • A gem color such as Reddish Pink becomes color Pink with modifier Reddish.

Review and Save

When the form opens, a notice lists anything you need to check before saving. None of these stop you from saving.

  • A value that doesn't match your settings, e.g. "OLD MINE BRILLIANT" does not match any shape value in your settings. Pick one before saving.
  • A field the report doesn't have, e.g. The lab report has no carat weight. Enter it before saving.
  • A gem type that isn't in your settings, e.g. Select the gem type before saving.
  • No certificate PDF, when IGI has none or it couldn't be downloaded. Upload it yourself on the certificate.
  • A certificate number already in your inventory, e.g. This certificate number is already on D1001.

The IGI certificate is locked

Certificate 1 keeps the IGI details exactly as the report has them. Its type, number, issue date and comments can't be edited, and it can't be removed. Its PDF can't be replaced, but if no PDF was attached you can upload one. You can still add more certificates.

Saving

Fill in the rest, such as Stock ID, price and ownership, and save as usual. The IGI certificate PDF is saved with the stock.
