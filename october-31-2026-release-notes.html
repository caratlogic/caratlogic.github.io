KSeF E-Invoicing for Poland — Release Notes

CaratLogic now connects directly to KSeF, Poland's National e-Invoice System (Krajowy System e-Faktur). Polish companies can submit invoices and credit note corrections to KSeF from CaratLogic, get the KSeF number, verification QR and official receipt (UPO) back, email the verified PDF to the buyer, and pull the purchase invoices issued to them into a new KSeF Inbox.

Connect CaratLogic to KSeF

Where: App Integrations → KSeF Config (admins only, for companies in Poland)

KSeF is Poland's national e-invoicing system. CaratLogic connects to it directly with your company's KSeF token. It doesn't go through Peppol or onFact.

Before you start

  • Sign in to KSeF as your company and create a KSeF system token.
  • Give the token permission to issue invoices (InvoiceWrite). If you'll use the KSeF Inbox, also give it permission to view invoices (InvoiceRead).
  • KSeF fixes a token's permissions when it's created. If a token is missing a permission, create a new token in KSeF.

Connect to KSeF

FieldNotes
Seller NIPRequired. Your company's 10-digit NIP, filled in from your company profile. The check digit is verified as you type. If it doesn't match the NIP in your company profile, a note tells you.
KSeF TokenRequired. Stored encrypted and never shown again. Use the eye icon to check what you typed.
Polish VAT StatusActive VAT payer or VAT exempt, with an optional Effective From date. Set to Active VAT payer when your company profile says you're VAT registered. Invoices can be submitted only when this is Active VAT payer.
VAT-UE StatusRegistered or Not registered, with an optional Effective From date.

Click Connect. CaratLogic checks the token with KSeF before saving it. A Connecting to KSeF message stays up while KSeF confirms, which can take up to a minute and a half. When it succeeds you'll see Connected to KSeF.

The KSeF Connection card

  • The header badge shows Connected, Not connected, Reconnect required or Error.
  • Once connected, the card shows Deployment (the KSeF environment your CaratLogic site uses), Seller NIP, Polish VAT status (e.g. Active VAT payer since 01 Jan 2026), VAT-UE status, Last authenticated and Session valid until.
  • If KSeF stops accepting the authorization, the badge changes to Reconnect required. Enter a valid token under Reconnect to KSeF and click Reconnect.
  • Reconnecting with a different NIP turns both KSeF capabilities off until an admin turns them on again.
  • Disconnect asks you to confirm, then removes the token from CaratLogic. The token stays valid in KSeF, so revoke it there if you no longer need it.
⚠ Connected doesn't prove the token can issue invoices. It means KSeF accepted the token for your company. The token's permissions are checked when you first submit an invoice, so if KSeF refuses that submission, check the token's permissions in KSeF.

NIP on Polish Business Contacts

Where: Business Contacts → Add Business Contact or Edit Business Contact, with Country set to Poland

For contacts in Poland, the VAT Number and VAT % fields are replaced by NIP and an Active VAT payer switch.

  • NIP takes exactly 10 digits. Spaces and dashes are removed as you type or paste.
  • The NIP check digit is verified, and a number made of one repeated digit, such as 1111111111, is rejected with This NIP is not valid. Please check the digits.
  • Turn on Active VAT payer when the contact is a VAT payer (czynny podatnik VAT). NIP is then required: NIP is required for an active VAT payer.
  • VAT % is no longer asked for Polish contacts. Polish VAT comes from Poland's VAT rate table.
  • When you change a contact's country, tax numbers that belonged to the old country (GSTIN, PAN or NIP) are removed when you save.
To submit an invoice to KSeF, the buyer needs a valid NIP with Active VAT payer turned on.

Submit an Invoice to KSeF

Where: Transactions → Invoices → open an invoice → KSeF card, or the invoice's row actions menu

Once your company has connected KSeF, final invoices show a KSeF card. Click Submit to KSeF. The Submit invoice to KSeF? dialog shows the invoice summary and reminds you that the invoice can't be cancelled while KSeF processes it or after acceptance. Click Submit.

  • CaratLogic sends KSeF a structured invoice (FA(3) XML) built from the invoice, and checks it against KSeF's official FA(3) format first. KSeF receives the structured invoice, not the PDF.
  • Your CaratLogic invoice number stays the invoice number. KSeF assigns its own KSeF number when it accepts the invoice, and both are shown.
  • The card shows Invoice number, KSeF number (Assigned on acceptance until then), Submitted, Accepted or Rejected, Last checked and Attempt, with the KSeF status in the header.
  • If the invoice can't be submitted yet, the card says why, e.g. Submitting to KSeF needs the Polish VAT status set to Active VAT payer in KSeF Config.

What KSeF submission supports in this release

RequirementDetails
SellerConnected to KSeF, with Polish VAT Status set to Active VAT payer.
BuyerA business contact in Poland with a valid NIP, Active VAT payer turned on, a company or contact name, and a street address, postal code and city.
CurrencyPLN
VATPoland's standard 23% rate on every line.
InvoiceA final, active invoice (not a draft or cancelled) with at least one line that has a description, quantity and price.
AdjustmentsNo discount, shipping or miscellaneous charges on the invoice.
Credit notesNot fully credited before it was sent. Fully credited invoices are not sent to KSeF.
⚠ Not supported yet. Invoices in other currencies, reduced, zero or exempt VAT, reverse charge, sales to buyers outside Poland, sales to consumers, invoices with a discount or shipping, and KSeF offline issuing. CaratLogic refuses these with the reason, and the invoice stays as it is.

Follow the KSeF Status

KSeF checks each invoice before it accepts it. The status badge on the KSeF card shows where it is and what to do next.

StatusWhat it meansWhat to do
Not submittedNever sent to KSeF.Submit to KSeF
QueuedPrepared, but not confirmed as sent.Reconcile to continue it.
Submitted / ProcessingKSeF is checking the invoice.Wait. The card updates by itself.
AcceptedKSeF accepted the invoice and assigned a KSeF number.Download the UPO and send the invoice to the buyer.
RejectedKSeF rejected the invoice. Its reason and code are shown. There's no KSeF number.Correct the invoice, then Submit again.
FailedThe submission ended without acceptance.Correct the invoice, then Submit again.
Submission uncertainKSeF hasn't confirmed whether it received the invoice.Reconcile. Don't submit it again.
Authorization requiredKSeF refused the saved authorization.An admin reconnects in KSeF Config, then Reconcile.
  • While KSeF is processing, the card checks the status every 15 seconds for up to two minutes. If KSeF takes longer, you'll see KSeF is taking longer than usual and a Check status button.
  • CaratLogic also checks unfinished submissions in the background every five minutes, so statuses update even when no one has the invoice open.
  • Reconcile looks up the saved submission in KSeF instead of sending the invoice again, so a slow or dropped connection never creates a second legal invoice.
  • Each new submission after a rejection counts as a new Attempt. Earlier attempts stay in the KSeF Submissions log.
⚠ Invoices are locked while in KSeF. While an invoice is Queued, Submitted, Processing, Submission uncertain or Authorization required, and once it's Accepted, it can't be edited or cancelled. After Rejected or Failed it can be edited again. To change an accepted invoice, create a credit note and submit it as a KSeF correction.

KSeF Verification PDF and Official Receipt (UPO)

Where: Invoice details → KSeF card, after the invoice is accepted

The verified PDF

  • When KSeF accepts the invoice, CaratLogic regenerates the invoice PDF. The CaratLogic QR code is replaced by the KSeF verification QR, with the KSeF number printed below it. Scanning the QR opens KSeF's verification page for the invoice.
  • The PDF is built from a copy of the invoice saved when it was submitted, so it always matches what KSeF accepted.
  • If the PDF couldn't be regenerated, the card shows PDF without KSeF verification. Click Regenerate KSeF PDF. This never sends the invoice to KSeF again.

The UPO

  • The UPO (Urzędowe Poświadczenie Odbioru) is KSeF's official receipt for the invoice. Click Download UPO to save it as an XML file, e.g. UPO-INV-26-0142.xml.
  • If KSeF hasn't provided the UPO yet, the card shows Official receipt (UPO) not retrieved yet. Click Check status to try again.

Send the KSeF Invoice to Your Buyer

Where: Invoice details → KSeF card → Buyer delivery

Once the invoice is accepted and its KSeF PDF is ready, click Send to buyer. The Send KSeF invoice to buyer dialog starts with the customer's email, and you can add more recipients. The buyer receives your usual invoice email with the KSeF-verified PDF and its KSeF number.

Delivery statusWhat it means
Not sentNo email has been sent yet.
SendingThe email is on its way.
SentThe email was sent. It can't be sent again from here.
FailedThe email wasn't sent. The reason is shown, and you can click Send again.
UncertainThe connection dropped after the email was handed over, so it may have arrived.
  • The panel shows the Recipients, Last attempt, when it was Sent, and a history of every delivery attempt.
  • Buyer delivery is tracked separately from KSeF. A failed email never changes the KSeF status or sends the invoice to KSeF again.
⚠ Uncertain deliveries need a check. The card shows Delivery needs operator review. The email can't be sent again from CaratLogic, so confirm with the buyer whether it arrived.

Submit a Credit Note as a KSeF Correction

Where: Transactions → Credit Notes → open a credit note → KSeF correction card, or the credit note's row actions menu → Submit correction

In KSeF, a credit note is recorded as a correction to the original invoice. Submitting it links it to the original invoice's KSeF number.

Which credit notes can be submitted

  • The credit note is linked to one original invoice that KSeF has already accepted. Otherwise you'll see e.g. The original invoice INV-26-0142 must be accepted by KSeF first.
  • It's in PLN, for a business contact in Poland with a valid NIP and Active VAT payer turned on.
  • Its lines are at Poland's 23% VAT, with no discount or shipping adjustments.

What you fill in

FieldNotes
Correction reasonRequired. Up to 256 characters, with a counter.
Correction effect typeRequired. Type 1: takes effect on the date of the original invoice. Type 2: takes effect on the date this correction is issued. Type 3: takes effect on another date, including different dates per line.

Click Submit in the Submit correction to KSeF? dialog.

The KSeF correction card

  • Shows Credit note number, Original invoice KSeF number, the correction's own KSeF number, Submitted, Accepted and Attempt, with the same statuses as invoices.
  • Actions: Check status, View PDF, Download UPO, and Submit again after a rejection. The reason and effect type you entered are kept for the next attempt.
  • When KSeF accepts the correction, the credit note PDF is regenerated with its KSeF number and verification QR.
⚠ The credit note's effects are already applied. Its balance, inventory and wallet effects are applied when the credit note is created, and they stay if KSeF rejects the correction.
Confirm the correction effect type with your Polish tax adviser before you submit.

KSeF Inbox for Received Invoices

Where: Financial Operations → KSeF Inbox

Purchase invoices that suppliers issue to your company in KSeF can now be pulled into CaratLogic for review.

Retrieving invoices

  • The Retrieval from KSeF card shows the sync status (Not started, Preparing, In progress, Completed or Failed), with Company NIP, Retrieved up to, Last sync, Active export and KSeF retry after.
  • For the first sync, pick Retrieve invoices from (today or earlier) and click Start sync. After that, click Sync now to fetch new invoices from where the last sync stopped.
  • KSeF prepares received invoices as an export. While it does, you'll see KSeF is preparing the export, the button reads Continue sync, and the page keeps checking by itself.
  • When it finishes you'll see e.g. Retrieved 12 received invoices. If more are waiting, sync again to continue.
  • If KSeF asks CaratLogic to wait, the sync button is disabled until the time shown in KSeF retry after.
  • Retrieval needs Retrieve received invoices turned on, a working KSeF connection, and a token with permission to view invoices. Only admins can sync. Everyone with access can view the inbox.

Received Invoices

  • Columns: Invoice (with its KSeF number), Seller (with its NIP), Issued, Gross and Review (New or Reviewed). Filter by Review status.
  • Row actions: Mark reviewed and Download XML, which saves the original invoice from KSeF, e.g. KSeF-<KSeF number>.xml.
  • Click a row to see the KSeF number, Invoice number, Gross amount, Seller, Seller NIP, Buyer NIP, Issue date, Received in KSeF, Retrieved and Reviewed.
  • Syncing again never creates duplicates. Each invoice is kept once, by its KSeF number.
⚠ Review only. Marking an invoice reviewed doesn't create a purchase, supplier balance, inventory or payment in CaratLogic. Record those the way you do today.

Changed

Polish VAT and NIP on Documents

  • Invoices for companies in Poland use Poland's standard VAT rate of 23% for gems, diamonds, jewellery and services, line by line. A different rate entered on the document isn't applied. Companies in other EU countries work as before.
  • On PDFs, a Polish company's tax number in the header is labelled NIP and shown as 10 digits, without the PL prefix.
  • A Polish buyer's tax number is also labelled NIP. Belgian and other buyers keep the VAT label, and Indian companies keep GSTIN.
