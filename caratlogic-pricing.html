The KYC Application

A separate compliance product that sits alongside the inventory system and shares the same login. Design your own due-diligence questionnaires, invite counterparties by email to a secure browser portal with no account to create, and move each submission through review to approval — with chasing, renewal and document-expiry alerts running on their own.

  • Build your own KYC formsSupplier, partner, broker or customer questionnaires with 15 question types.
  • Ready-made supplier templateAn eight-page questionnaire covering licences, financials, supply chain and RJC compliance.
  • Email invites, no loginContacts complete the form in a secure browser portal with a one-time emailed passcode.
  • Secure document uploadLicences, certificates and incorporation papers go straight into private encrypted storage.
  • Ten-stage review workflowMove each submission to Approved, Conditionally Approved, Rejected or More Info Required.
  • Automated chase & renewalUnfinished KYC is chased on your cadence; documents raise expiry alerts before they lapse.
